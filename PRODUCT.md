# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SICAP.ai serves three audiences who all arrive needing the same thing: the right procurement record, found fast.

- **Businesses and bidders (primary).** Companies scanning open `licitații` and `achiziții` for opportunities worth bidding on, sizing up the `autoritate contractantă` behind a tender, and watching what competitors win. They search often and value speed over exploration.
- **Journalists and transparency watchdogs (secondary).** Reporters and anti-corruption groups tracing where public money goes, building a case from a company's or an authority's contract history.
- **Researchers and analysts (secondary).** Academics, NGOs, and data analysts studying procurement patterns across the whole dataset.

Expertise is mixed: procurement professionals who know the jargon sit alongside reporters and citizens who do not. The product must read clearly to both. The recurring context is a focused, return-often lookup, not a leisurely browse.

## Product Purpose

A search engine for Romanian public procurement, "Sistem Inteligent de Căutare Achiziții Publice." It indexes the data published on e-licitatie.ro (public tenders, direct acquisitions, offline acquisitions) and makes it genuinely findable: by keyword, by company (`firmă` / CUI), and by contracting authority.

It exists because the official source is slow and hard to use. SICAP.ai's reason to exist is being dramatically faster and clearer than the system it draws from. Success is simple: a user lands, searches, and reaches the tender, company, or authority they needed in seconds, then comes back next time.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **fast, trustworthy, clear.**

- **Fast and efficient.** The interface gets out of the way. Search is the act, results are the reward, friction is the enemy.
- **Authoritative and trustworthy.** The data is the source of truth. Credibility comes from accuracy and real numbers shown plainly, never from embellishment.
- **Clear and approachable.** Plain language over bureaucratic jargon wherever possible, so a journalist or citizen is as capable here as a procurement officer.

Feel references (direction, not literal styling): **Linear** for crisp, opinionated, keyboard-fast minimalism, and **Perplexity** for a search-first layout where the query and its results are the entire stage. The voice is matter-of-fact and civic-minded: informative, never salesy, never cute.

## Anti-references

What SICAP.ai must never become:

- **Government-portal bloat (the sharpest one).** e-licitatie.ro itself: cluttered, slow, bureaucratic, buried navigation. Being the opposite of this is the whole point.
- **Hypey SaaS marketing.** Gradient blobs, hero-metric templates, growth-hack landing energy, emoji enthusiasm.
- **Consumer-playful.** Mascots, jokey microcopy, gamified flourishes. Public money is a serious subject.
- **Generic AI-dashboard sameness.** Identical card grids, purple gradients, the template-y "modern" look that signals nothing was actually decided.

## Design Principles

1. **Search is the hero.** The product lives or dies on "find the right tender fast." Every screen serves discovery and the path to a record; nothing competes with the query for attention.
2. **Beat the source.** The benchmark is e-licitatie.ro, and the bar is to be obviously faster, cleaner, and easier to navigate. When a choice is unclear, pick the one that frustrates someone less than the official portal does.
3. **Let the data earn the trust.** Show real counts, accurate figures, and honest states. Credibility is built by being right and being plain, not by decoration or persuasion.
4. **Legible to outsiders, powerful for regulars.** Default to plain language a non-expert can follow, while keeping the depth (filters, entity history, raw figures) a daily user relies on. One screen should not force a trade-off between the two.
5. **Restraint over flourish.** Favor calm density and deliberate choices over visual noise. If an element is not helping someone find or trust a record, it justifies its presence or it goes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: sufficient color contrast in both light and dark themes, complete keyboard navigation (critical for the search-often power user), visible focus states, and proper labels for screen readers on search inputs, filters, and data tables. Romanian-language content must keep its diacritics (ă, â, î, ș, ț) intact and legible. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for any added motion, even though it is not a formal requirement at this bar.
